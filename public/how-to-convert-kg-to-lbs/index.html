How to Convert KG to LBS — Formula & Step-by-Step Guide

1 kilogram (kg) = 2.20462 pounds (lbs)

The Formula

To convert kilograms to pounds, multiply the number of kilograms by 2.20462:

Pounds = Kilograms × 2.20462

Step-by-Step Example

Convert 5 kg to lbs:

  1. Take the weight in kilograms: 5 kg
  2. Multiply by the conversion factor: 5 × 2.20462
  3. Result: 11.02 lbs

How to Convert LBS to KG (Reverse)

To convert pounds back to kilograms, divide by 2.20462:

Kilograms = Pounds ÷ 2.20462

Example: 150 lbs ÷ 2.20462 = 68.04 kg

Why Convert Between KG and LBS?

Kilograms (kg) are the standard unit of mass in the metric system, used in most countries worldwide. Pounds (lbs) belong to the imperial system, primarily used in the United States. Common scenarios requiring kg-to-lbs conversion include: fitness and body weight tracking, international shipping, cooking recipes, medical dosages, and sports weight classes.
